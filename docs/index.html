What we tested

We tested whether a private roster of 45 Solana operator wallets bought tokens in the 7 days before Nansen smart-money netflow onsets more often than matched quiet tokens on the same day: 56 pairs over 90 days. Against the pre-registered market-cap-matched null the roster looked early, 18/56 vs 4/56 (4.5×). Matching on token age instead, a control we added after seeing that result, the lift falls to 1.8× (18/56 vs 10/56, p = 0.13, not significant): the roster buys young tokens, and young tokens are where onsets happen. What is left is concentrated in two wallets; without them the age-matched lift is 1.0× (8/56 vs 8/56). This covers this cohort only and says nothing about smart money in general.

How it works

  1. Onset: the first day a token's Nansen smart-money netflow reaches $10k after a quiet week.
  2. Hit: a roster wallet's token amount rose in the 7 days before the onset, read from stored 90-day balance history.
  3. Null: a quiet token on the same day, matched on market cap (pre-registered) or token age (post hoc), same window and hit rule.
  4. Lift = spike hit rate ÷ null hit rate; p from Fisher's exact test. Median lead 120h, set by the 7-day window.

Nansen endpoints used: token-screener/historical (trader_type=sm, the onset clock), profiler/address/historical-balances (entry history), profiler/address/current-balance (live snapshots).
